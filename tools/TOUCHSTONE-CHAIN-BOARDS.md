# Touchstone chain training-board audit

Reviewed 2026-08-31 against Touchstone's current official
[training-board guide](https://touchstoneclimbing.com/touchstone-training-boards/)
and the named branch pages. This is a bounded chain audit, not a general claim
that Touchstone operates every installation of any listed board system.

## Result

| System | Current official branch inventory | Production disposition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Touchstone Board | Team Training Center, Pacific Pipe, Class 5, Hyperion; The Post confirmed by its current branch page | Five current boards retained. The four registry rows and The Post all receive the official fixed 35-degree, LED-equipped system properties. Class 5's duplicate point is merged onto its Kilter/Tension venue. |
| Kilter Board | Diablo Rock Gym, Great Western Power Company, Pacific Pipe, Team Training Center, Dogpatch Boulders, Verdigo Boulders, Class 5 | All seven already present. The guide states that all are adjustable 12×12 Original boards, correcting Great Western Power Company's obsolete 14×12 registry size. |
| MoonBoard | Team Training Center (2019), The Oaks (2024), Pacific Pipe (2016), Cliffs of Id (2024), The Post (2019), The Studio (2024), Hyperion (2024) | The Oaks and The Studio already existed. The other five are restored from current primary evidence because the frozen MoonBoard registry omits them. Cliffs of Id's branch amenities page also states its fixed 40-degree angle. |
| Tension Board | The Post (TB1 mirror, fixed), Pacific Pipe (TB1 mirror, fixed), Team Training Center (TB2 8×12 mirror, fixed), MetalMark (TB2 12×12 spray, adjustable), Class 5 (TB2 12×12 mirror), Hyperion (TB2 12×12 spray) | All six already present; no curated row needed. |

The guide's MoonBoard heading says “six locations” but the same current page
names seven distinct branches. The audit preserves the seven explicit rows and
records the copy/count contradiction rather than silently choosing one number.

## Exclusions and boundaries

- Aisle 19's Touchstone Board and Pipeworks' Kilter Board are explicitly
  “coming soon”; neither is published as a current installation.
- The Post is absent from the central Touchstone Board subsection, but its
  current official [branch page](https://touchstoneclimbing.com/the-post/) and
  [tour page](https://touchstoneclimbing.com/the-post/tour/) still identify the
  system. It therefore remains current rather than being removed from a
  secondary omission alone.
- Class 5's Touchstone registry point lies about 10 m from its address-bearing
  Kilter/Tension point and rounds into a separate venue key. The official
  [branch page](https://touchstoneclimbing.com/class-5/) identifies one branch,
  and the training-board guide places all three systems there. The old point is
  a backed `duplicate`; a curated Touchstone row at the surviving coordinate
  preserves the board without preserving the duplicate marker.
- Cliffs of Id's coordinate comes from the map pin embedded by its own official
  [branch page](https://touchstoneclimbing.com/cliffs-of-id/), not from a map
  directory. That page also provides the venue identity, exact address and
  complete regular week; its
  [amenities page](https://touchstoneclimbing.com/cliffs-of-id/tour-and-amenities/)
  supplies the board model and angle.

No “coming soon” row, inferred branch, directory schedule or search-result
snippet is admitted to production.
