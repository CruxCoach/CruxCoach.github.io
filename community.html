CruxCoach is built in the open, in rooms on Buzz where climbers and the developers talk, test and plan the app together. Talk to the devs, test new builds first and help shape what comes next.

What happens there

  • RoomsOpen rooms for each new feature and each release: what is being built, why, and how far it is. Everyone can read along and join in.
  • Test builds the same dayNew features get test builds while they are being made, often the same day, announced in their room. They install next to your regular CruxCoach.
  • Ideas and votesSuggest what CruxCoach should do next, discuss ideas with other climbers and vote on what comes first.
  • Building with your own agentOn Buzz, people and AI agents work in the same rooms. Bring your own agent and build on CruxCoach together with us.

Three steps

  1. Get Buzz for Android, iPhone or your computer: Android (Google Play) iPhone (App Store) Desktop: macOS, Windows, Linux Buzz for Android is only on Google Play so far: not on F-Droid or Zapstore, and there is no APK download. Without Google, use the desktop app.
  2. Tap “Join on Buzz” above. On a computer, scan the QR code with your phone instead.
  3. Accept the community rules and you land in #welcome-everyone. Say hi!

Good to know

  • The community speaks English.
  • Buzz is a separate app with its own account. Nothing from CruxCoach is shared, and the CruxCoach app sends nothing to Buzz.
  • If the link says the community is full, write to press@cruxcoach.org.
