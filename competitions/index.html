Competitions

Run a competition on your board, or take part in one. There is no account and no server: the competition, every entry and every result live on Nostr, signed by the people who made them.

Organize

Create a competition, share one link, decide who takes part, and run the queue from your phone. Everything you decide is signed with your key and is permanent.

Take part

Open the link the organizer shared, register, and see whose turn it is, how many attempts you have left and where you stand — on any phone, without installing anything.

Live screen

A read-only screen for a projector or a tablet by the entrance. It holds no key, so it is safe to leave running and safe to share.

How it works

A competition is a signed document on Nostr. The organizer publishes decisions as an append-only, linked record; every client replays that record and arrives at the same standings. Nobody has to trust our servers, because there are none.

What is public

Your public key, the name you choose for the screen, your division, and your results. Not your real name, not your contact details, and nothing about anyone who only looked. The name you pick appears on a public screen and on public relays — a nickname is fine.

The Android app

CruxCoach for Android drives the board itself over Bluetooth and has the same competition view built in.
